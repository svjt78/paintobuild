# Legal and identity notes

Recorded 27 September 2026. **This is general information, not legal or tax advice.** Rules vary by state and country and change over time. Check anything important with a lawyer or accountant.

Situation: you're based in the US, operate under the PainToBuild name, show only hello@paintobuild.com on the site, and are open to clients anywhere in the world.

## The website

- US federal law doesn't generally require a service business to show its owner's name or address on its website.
- Staying under the PainToBuild name with only an email address is common for US solo businesses. The current pages can go live as they are.

## Where your identity does matter

1. **Marketing emails (CAN-SPAM).** The template signup sends occasional updates. US law requires commercial emails to include a valid physical mailing address. MailerLite requires one in the footer, so one is probably already set.
   - **Action:** check which address MailerLite uses. If it's your home, consider a PO box or virtual mailbox.
2. **Client agreements and invoices.** A paying client should know who they're contracting with.
   - **Action:** put your legal name, or a registered business name, on the agreement template and invoices, even though the website stays under PainToBuild.
3. **Trading under "PainToBuild" (DBA).** Most states expect you to register a "doing business as" name when you trade under a name that isn't your legal name. Banks and payment services often ask for it.
   - **Action:** check your state and county rules before taking review payments under the PainToBuild name.

## Clients outside the US

- **EU and UK disclosure rules** (such as Germany's "Impressum") mainly apply to businesses established there. A US solo adviser working with founders there usually isn't bound by them in the same way, but it isn't settled for every case.
- **GDPR** can apply to a non-EU business that offers services to people in the EU. The privacy page already covers what is collected, which services handle it, and access and deletion requests.
  - **Action:** if EU or UK clients become regular, ask a lawyer whether you need an EU or UK representative or anything else.
- **Tax.** Gumroad handles sales tax and VAT on the guides, as seller of record.
  - **Action:** for reviews you invoice yourself, check with an accountant once you have clients outside the US.

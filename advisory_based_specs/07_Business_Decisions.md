# Business decisions for the advisory site

Recorded 27 September 2026. These are the facts and choices behind the website copy, especially the privacy and terms pages. If any of them change, the pages must change too.

## Decided before this work

- Advisory is the main business. It covers three areas: finding a problem to work on, deciding what to build, and deciding what to try next.
- There is one starting service, the **Founder Decision Review**: one product or idea, one decision, a written review, one round of questions and one later review of the test results.
- There is no public price. The work, fee and timing are agreed in writing before work starts. The old $99 and $349 prices are retired.
- The Builder Validation Brief ($19) and Challenge Mining Kit ($39) stay as secondary products for people doing their own research. The free template stays as a secondary resource.
- Everyday Default Audits is off the main journey. `/everyday/` stays as a quiet legacy page.
- The site uses "I" for you and "you" for the visitor.

## Defaults you accepted

- **Cancellation:** if you or the client cancel before work starts, the client pays nothing, and anything already paid is refunded.
- **Guide refunds:** the Kit follows the same rule as the Brief. Sales are final, apart from legal rights and Gumroad's buyer protection.

## Defaults you did not adopt

- **A fixed deletion period** (such as 12 months). The privacy page promises only deletion on request.
- **An automatic lapse of the later review** when no results arrive. This is left to each client's agreement instead.

## Left to each client's written agreement

The terms page says these are set in the agreement:

- Calls or written work only.
- Limits on material reviewed, product testing and new research.
- Delivery date.
- How long the client has to send questions and test results, and what happens if no test is run.
- How and when payment is made, and when work counts as started.
- Cancellation and refunds after work starts.

Suggestion: keep a short agreement template that covers each of these. The terms page also says the agreement overrides the page where they differ.

## Promises the live site makes

You need to keep all of these:

1. One round of questions and one later review of results per Founder Decision Review.
2. The work, price and delivery date agreed in writing before starting.
3. No charge if you or the client cancel before work starts.
4. Client material isn't shared with other people without agreement, and isn't published without separate permission.
5. No client material in public AI services.
6. Deletion of messages and review material on request.
7. No guarantee of customers, sales, revenue or funding. The site never promises these.
8. The client can use their written review for their own business.

## Still open, not blocking the first publish

- Capacity (how many reviews at once) and typical turnaround. Add these to the site only when you can meet them reliably.
- A price, once one is decided. Don't add one to the site or Gumroad until then.
- Professional background or credentials. Add them only when they can be supported.
